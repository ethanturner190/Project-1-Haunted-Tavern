Game Rules

How to play each game.

Gwent

A strategy card game where two players play cards to the table. Each card has a value that stacks with other the other friendly cards played. Highest amount of points at the end of the round wins the round. The first player to win two rounds wins the game. (Uses a custom deck of cards, not standard 52)

The table has 3 rows per player that represent close, medium and far range. Each unit card fits into one of these ranges. There are special cards that have abilities such as doubling the amount of points each card is worth on a row. The round keeps going until both players run out of cards or both players pass. When a player passes they will not be able to play anymore cards that round.

How to Play

  1. Each player starts with a hand of 10 cards.
  2. Players take turns playing cards on the table one at a time.
  3. Each card has a value that adds up to other cards.
  4. Players can pass when they do not want to play more cards.
  5. The player with the most points wins the round.
  6. The first player to win two rounds wins the game.

Strategy

Don't play every card the first round without reason. Having more cards than the other player gives you an advantage. Sometimes it is better to lose one round rather than play all your cards.

High Card

Very simple game. Two players draw a card and play them. The player with the highest card wins the round.

How to Play

  1. Each player draws one card from a standard 52 card deck.
  2. The player with the higher card wins.
  3. If both cards have the same value each player draws again.

Card Values

Number cards are worth their number. Jack is 11, Queen is 12, King is 13 and Ace is 14.

Blackjack

Blackjack is a game where the dealer is playing every other player independently, but at the same time. Each player wants their cards to add to 21 but without going over. Each player's cards are visible to everyone else. One of the dealer's cards is hidden.

How to Play

  1. The player and dealer each start with two cards.
  2. Both player cards are known and the dealer hides one.
  3. The player can hit to get another card.
  4. The player can stand to keep their current hand.
  5. Going over 21 means the player loses.
  6. The hand closest to 21 wins.
  7. The dealer will draw a card until they at least hold 17.
  8. An equal score will result in a tie

Card Values

Number cards are worth their number. Jack, Queen, and King are worth 10. Ace can be worth 1 or 11.
